Information used on your phone

You choose what to enter: income, expenses, amounts, dates, descriptions, categories, bills, budgets, savings goals and debt details. Debt details may include a name, remaining principal, interest rate, instalment, due date and the fee you enter. Your language preference is stored separately.

The app uses this information for budgets, reports, category and recurring-payment suggestions, available-money estimates and purchase or debt-repayment scenarios. Calculations use local rules and formulas on your phone. Your data is not sent to an AI service.

This version requires no account, does not connect to banks and does not access SMS messages, contacts, photos or location. It has no internet permission, proprietary server, ads, analytics or automatic external crash reporting. FructoseLabs does not automatically receive, sell or distribute the financial data you enter in Rost.

Storage and protection

Your information is stored in the app's private storage, protected by Android's mechanisms. The app does not add its own encryption to the financial file and does not currently provide its own PIN or biometric lock. Protection also depends on your device's security. Use a screen lock and keep exported backups in a safe place.

Android automatic app backup is disabled in the app's configuration. We do not provide device-to-device synchronisation or keep a copy from which we could recover lost data.

Exports, imports and providers you choose

Export happens only when you request it, through Android's document picker, to the destination you select. JSON backups include financial data and debts; CSV exports include transactions. Your language preference is not part of the financial backup. Exported files are plain text, without app-provided encryption or a password.

If you choose a cloud provider or later share a file, that provider or recipient handles it under its own terms. We cannot control or delete those copies. Import reads the file you select and replaces local data only after confirmation.

Retention and deletion

Local financial data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app, use Settings → Delete all data, clear the app's storage in Android or uninstall the app. The language preference may remain after a financial reset; clearing Android app storage or uninstalling also removes that preference. There is no online account to delete.

Exported copies remain at the destinations you selected and must be deleted separately, including any trash folders or copies held by external providers. FructoseLabs cannot restore locally deleted information without a backup you supply.

If you email us

We receive your email address, message and any files you choose to send. We use them to handle your request through Google's Gmail email service. Contacting us does not automatically send your financial database. Avoid sending account numbers, banking details or full financial backups unless necessary.
